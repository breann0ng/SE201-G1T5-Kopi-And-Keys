Fresh Start Housing Scheme

Learn about the Fresh Start Housing Scheme that helps public rental families own their next home.

The Fresh Start Housing Scheme (Fresh Start) helps ComLink+ families with young children living in public rental flats to own their next homes. Through a combination of financial assistance and social support, you can buy a 2-room Flexi or 3-room Standard flat on a shorter lease.

Interested households need to apply for Fresh Start before applying for a flat.

Key features

Here is a summary of the scheme's key features:

Flat types and lease options

You can choose from:

  • 2-room Flexi
  • 3-room Standard flat

These flats come with shorter leases, making them more affordable than 99-year lease flats.

You can select a lease length between 45 and 65 years, which must last until you and your spouse reach 95 years old.
Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) 20 years
HDB concessionary housing loan You may apply for the loan, subject to credit assessment.
Resale levy Your resale levy will be capped at $30,000. This amount will be reduced based your chosen lease length under Fresh Start.
Housing grants

If you are a first-timer family applicant, you can receive the Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) of up to $120,000.

If you are a second-timer family applicant, you can receive the Fresh Start Housing Grant (FSHG) in 2 parts:

  1. An upfront grant disbursed shortly before key collection
  2. A deferred grant disbursed over 5 years after key collection

The FSHG amount depends on application timing as follows:

Application Timing Upfront Grant Before Key Collection Deferred Grant Over 5 Years Total Grant Amount
Before July 2025 $35,000 Up to $15,000 $50,000
From July 2025 $60,000 Up to $15,000 $75,000
Priority allocation You may receive priority for up to 10% of 2-room Flexi and 3-room BTO/ SBF flats under the Tenants’ Priority Scheme (TPS).

How to apply

If you meet the eligibility conditions, you can apply for the Fresh Start Housing Scheme.

Eligibility conditions

You and all listed applicants and occupiers must meet the eligibility conditions.

You and your family members must meet these conditions to qualify for Fresh Start:

  • You and your spouse (if applicable) must be aged 35 to below 55
  • You or your spouse, and at least 1 child (aged below 21) must be Singapore Citizens
  • You or your spouse (if applicable) must have stable employment for the last 12 months
  • Your average gross monthly household income must not exceed $8,000
  • Your household members do not own other properties overseas or locally, and have not disposed of any in the last 30 months
  • Your household has not previously received any subsidy under the Relocation, Sale of Flat to Sitting Tenants, or Rent & Purchase Schemes
  • Your household has occupied a public rental flat for at least 1 year, with less than 3 months of rental arrears in the last 12 months
  • You must qualify for the Letter of Social Assessment (LSA) from HDB which considers family stability, employment status, finances, and school attendance of the children. The LSA is subject to yearly renewal, until 5 years after key collection.

Other things to note

  • You must apply for a flat within the first year of your LSA to remain on Fresh Start
  • You must renew your LSA yearly to collect the keys to your flat booked under Fresh Start. Otherwise, you may have to forfeit 5% of the flat price.
  • The maximum HDB loan you can take is 75% of the flat price, based on prevailing Loan-to-Value limits
  • At least 10% of the flat price must be paid using your CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings and/or cash. First-timers may use the Enhanced Housing Grant (EHG) for this.
  • The remaining flat price can be paid using your CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings and/or cash. Second-timers may use the upfront Fresh Start Housing Grant for this.
  • Second-timers will receive the deferred Fresh Start Housing Grant over 5 years after key collection. The yearly disbursement is subject to the renewal of your LSA.
  • Your family may need to attend face-to-face interviews to renew your LSA
