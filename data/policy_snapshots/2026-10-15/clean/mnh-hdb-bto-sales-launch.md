# June 2026 Sales Launch

6,952 BTO flats in Ang Mo Kio, Bishan, Bukit Merah, Sembawang and Woodlands have been launched.

About 6,952 BTO flats in Ang Mo Kio, Bishan, Bukit Merah, Sembawang and Woodlands are on offer at the June 2026 Sales Launch, giving home buyers a wide range of options to choose from.

Families with three or more children can also look forward to added support when applying for a flat, with the allocation quota under the Third Child Priority Scheme (TCPS) increased from up to 5% to up to 10% from this sales launch onwards.

If you’re looking to move into your home sooner, you may consider the 2,035 Shorter Waiting Time (SWT) flats at Sembawang Portico and Sembawang Brook, which have waiting times of less than 3 years.

For seniors and first-timer singles, about 1 in 3 units on offer are 2-room Flexi flats, providing you with more options to find the right home.

New HDB flats are classified as Standard, Plus or Prime projects, depending on their locational attributes. Flats in Standard projects come with the least restrictions, while Plus and Prime flats have higher subsidies but also come with tighter restrictions. Read more about HDB flat classification.

⭐**You must have a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter before you can apply for a flat during an HDB sales launch.**

It takes about a month or more during peak periods to process an HFE letter, after HDB receives all the required documents. To participate in the June 2026 Sales Launch, you should have applied for your HFE letter and submitted all required documents by 15 May 2026.

**Housing Support for BTO Flat Buyers**

- **Enhanced CPF Housing Grant:** Eligible first-timer couples buying a BTO flat may receive an Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) of up to $120,000. Eligible first-timer singles buying a BTO flat can also receive an EHG of up to $60,000.
- **Priority for first-timer families:** A higher proportion of the flat supply is set aside for first-timer families. They also enjoy an additional ballot chance.
  - Married couples and parents with young children who qualify for the First-Timer (Parents & Married Couples) category can enjoy extra support to help them secure a flat more quickly.
- **Priority Schemes**: HDB’s priority schemes help first-timers and those with more urgent housing needs to secure a flat.

**Useful Tips for Applying for a BTO Flat**

- **Submit your flat application via the HDB Flat Portal.** As all the information on the flats on offer is available on the HDB Flat Portal, you do not have to visit HDB Hub.
- **You can only submit 1 application for 1 flat type and 1 location** during each sales launch.
- **Applicants are shortlisted via a computer ballot and applications are not processed on a first-come-first-served basis.** You do not need to rush to apply within the first few hours or even the first few days of the application period – just make sure you apply before the application period ends.
- **Boost your chances of success by applying for projects with lower application rates.** You can check the application rates on the HDB Flat Portal, which are updated several times a day throughout the application period.

## Ang Mo Kio: Kebun Baru Breeze and Kebun Baru Ridge

There’s still plenty more that Ang Mo Kio has to offer to new home buyers, with 2 new BTO projects coming to the town! Kebun Baru Breeze is bounded by Ang Mo Kio Avenue 1 and Ang Mo Kio Rise, while Kebun Baru Ridge is located along Ang Mo Kio Avenue 2.

Whether you’re grabbing a meal, staying active or simply unwinding after a long day, plenty of amenities are located nearby. Explore the food and shopping options at Mayflower Shopping & Food Centre, Kebun Baru Market & Food Centre, Ang Mo Kio Town Centre, and more. For nature lovers, enjoy the expansive greenery at Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, or take a stroll and cycle along the scenic ABC Waters @ Kallang River. Opportunities for sports, recreation and community activities are also close at hand, with facilities such as the ActiveSG Sport Park @ Teck Ghee and Kebun Baru Community Club nearby.

Flats at Kebun Baru Ridge are Shorter Waiting Time flats, with a waiting time of about 3 years and 1 month.

## Bishan: Lakeview Cascadia

For the first time in over four decades, new homes are coming to the Lakeview area in Bishan – giving more young families the opportunity to live close to their parents, while refreshing and rejuvenating the neighbourhood for current and future residents alike. This is the first BTO project to be launched in Lakeview, with two other projects to be launched in the next two years.

Lakeview Cascadia gives you easy access to a wide range of amenities nearby – from food options at Shunfu Mart and shopping malls in the bustling Bishan Town Centre, to everyday conveniences at nearby coffee shops and heartland stores. If you’re looking to stay active, enjoy a leisurely stroll at the scenic Upper Thomson Park Connector and MacRitchie Reservoir, or make use of the recreational facilities at Bishan Sport Centre.

## Bukit Merah: Berlayar Rise

When plans for Berlayar estate were unveiled back in September 2025, the announcement was met with plenty of buzz – and with good reason! The new estate marks the start of the transformation of the Greater Southern Waterfront, which brings vibrant city living within reach for all Singaporeans.

Berlayar Rise is the second to be launched at Berlayar estate. Located near nature spots such as Labrador Nature Reserve, Berlayar Creek, and the Southern Ridges, the project will offer residents a unique experience of harbourfront living close to nature. Projects at Berlayar estate are designed with staggered block heights, to maximise scenic views of the Southern Ridges and the coastline. Residents will also enjoy good connectivity, with Telok Blangah MRT and Labrador Park MRT stations nearby, while VivoCity is just a short trip away for shopping and dining.

## Sembawang: Sembawang Brook and Sembawang Portico

Located in the north of Singapore, Sembawang offers its residents a well-connected and convenient living environment, so residents of Sembawang Brook and Sembawang Portico will be spoilt for choice with the wide range of amenities available. From shopping and dining options at Sun Plaza and Sembawang Mart to everyday essentials at nearby heartland shops, as well as opportunities for recreation and outdoor activities at the integrated development Bukit Canberra and the Sembawang Park Connector, you’ll have all you need!

Flats at both Sembawang Brook and Sembawang Portico are Shorter Waiting Time flats, with waiting times of less than 3 years.
