# Single Premium Home Protection Scheme cover

Members insured under the HPS before 1 March 2001 are covered under the Single Premium until they turn 55 or 60, depending on when they joined the scheme. The maximum cover age is stated on the Single Premium certificate issued to members when their Single Premium covers commence. Single Premium premiums are paid in a lump sum.

Annual Premium covers are issued from 1 March 2001 and cover members until they turn 65. Members under Annual Premium pay their premiums annually.

Your Single Premium (SP) HPS may be converted to an Annual Premium (AP) HPS if there are any changes to your loan repayment period or loan amount, including refinancing of an existing loan. However, any consequential increase to your HPS cover, e.g. increase in cover period or sum assured, is subject to you being in good health. You will remain insured under your SP cover if you are not eligible for the additional AP cover.

If you’re covered under Single Premium cover at age 55 or 60, it’ll be converted to an Annual Premium cover if you still have an outstanding loan.

Your Single Premium cover will end at either age 55 or 60. The Annual Premium cover continues the insurance protection until you turn 65 or your housing loan is fully paid, whichever comes first.

If you’re insured under the new Annual Premium scheme, you can continue to use your CPF savings to pay monthly instalments even after your Single Premium cover has ended.

When you convert from a Single Premium to an Annual Premium cover, you must pay an annual premium for the cover extension and subsequent renewal of your Annual Premium cover.


The new Annual Premium cover is based on the outstanding housing loan amount when your Single Premium cover ends.

Get exempted from the Annual Premium scheme if you already have an equivalent private life insurance that covers your outstanding housing loan.

If you’re not using your CPF savings to pay monthly housing instalments and no longer need the financial protection, you can apply to end your Annual Premium cover.
